BrainRec is a screen and camera recording app for macOS, published by Let's Code Brain ("we", "us").

## The short version
BrainRec does not collect, store on our servers, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It does not connect to the internet.

## What BrainRec uses and why
- **Screen Recording** – to record the display or window you choose.
- **Camera** – to record your face next to your screen.
- **Microphone** – to record your voice.
- **System audio** – only if you turn it on, to record sound playing on your Mac.
- **A folder you choose** – to save your recordings.

Each of these is requested through the standard macOS permission prompts. You can turn any permission off at any time in
System Settings → Privacy & Security.

## Where your recordings go
Recordings are saved only to the folder on your Mac that you pick (or BrainRec's default folder). We never receive them.
Deleting the files deletes the recordings. BrainRec does not upload anything and has no cloud storage.

## What we do not do
- No account or sign-up
- No analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party SDKs
- No network connection of any kind

## What is stored on your Mac
Your app settings (for example chosen camera, quality, layout and output folder) are stored locally by macOS in BrainRec's
preferences. They never leave your Mac.

## Children
BrainRec does not collect any information from anyone, including children.
